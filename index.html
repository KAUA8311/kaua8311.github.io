🌸 What is Sakura Print?

Sakura Print is a simple, friendly application that lets you print photos, documents, and web pages from your phone, just as easily as you would from a computer. It connects your phone to a shared home printer, making it feel like magic. No more transferring files to a laptop, no more plugging in cables, and no more complicated setup. Just tap, print, and go.

It works beautifully with Linux-based systems, CUPS (the standard printing system on Linux), and AirPrint (Apple's wireless printing technology). Whether you have an Android, iPhone, or any other modern smartphone, Sakura Print bridges the gap between your phone and your printer.

🖥️ What You'll Need

Sakura Print is designed to work on most home computers and printers. Here's what we recommend for a smooth experience:

  • ✅ A computer running Linux (Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora, or any modern Linux distribution) that will stay connected to your printer.
  • ✅ A home printer that supports CUPS or AirPrint (most modern wireless printers from HP, Canon, Epson, and Brother work perfectly).
  • ✅ A smartphone (Android, iPhone, or tablet) connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your computer and printer.
  • ✅ About 50 MB of free space on your computer for installation.

💡 Good to know: If your printer is already connected to your Linux computer via USB or Wi-Fi, Sakura Print will likely detect it instantly and work right away.

🛠️ I Have a Problem - What Should I Do?

Even the smoothest tools sometimes need a little help. Here are answers to the most common questions we hear from first-time users:

❓ My printer isn't showing up.

Make sure your printer is turned on and connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your computer. Sometimes restarting both the printer and the computer helps. Give it a few seconds after opening Sakura Print — it's still looking.

❓ The print is coming out blurry or small.

This usually means the print settings on your phone were set to a lower quality. In the print dialog, look for "Quality" or "Paper Size" options and make sure they are set to "Best" or "A4/Letter" depending on your preference.

❓ Does it work with my iPhone?

Absolutely! Since Sakura Print supports AirPrint, iPhones and iPads will find your printer automatically, just like they would with an Apple-approved printer. Same goes for Android — it works with Google's built-in printing tools.

💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sakura Print really free?

Yes, completely free and open source. No hidden costs, no premium version, no ads. It's made by people who believe printing should be easy for everyone.

Can I print from my phone if my printer is connected via USB?

Yes! As long as the printer is connected to the computer running Sakura Print (via USB or wireless), you can print from your phone. Your phone talks to the computer, and the computer talks to the printer.

Will it work on my Windows computer?

While Sakura Print is specifically designed for Linux systems, many users have successfully used it on Windows with a little technical tweaking. For a seamless experience, we recommend using it on Linux as intended.

Is my data safe?

Your documents are processed locally on your network. Nothing is sent to the cloud or to any third-party server. Your privacy is respected.
